A little lunch at Arbuckle.

A personal browser game inspired by Stanford GSB’s Arbuckle Dining Pavilion. The station arrangement follows Mason’s description. Dimensions, seating positions, menu dishes, pricing, satisfaction values, and character likenesses are approximations for this game.

Lilybeth’s hairstyle, uniform, friendly personality, and greeting are based on Mason’s description; the character is an artistic representation.

Architectural references

  • Stanford GSB: Dining
  • RMA Studio: Knight Management Center — dining-room reference photography.
  • Arbuckle interior station reference

Reference photos informed the modeled architecture and are not embedded in the game.

Character assets

Female Adult 05, Female Adult 08, Male Adult 01, and idle animations from Microsoft Rocketbox. Copyright © 2020 Microsoft. Used under the MIT license. Texture files resized and encoded as WebP. Character rendering, placement, poses, and Lilybeth’s apron customized for this game.
